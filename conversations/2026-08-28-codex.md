Their AppView-specific community-post architecture was treated as service-decomposition precedent, not as universal authority over another PDS's records.
- [str4d/plc](https://github.com/str4d/plc) demonstrates focused PLC key inspection and audit validation with a planned hardware-backed backup-key workflow.
- The official [PLC specification](https://github.com/did-method-plc/did-method-plc) and [PLC read-replica guidance](https://atproto.com/blog/plc-replicas) support cryptographically verified histories and plurality of read sources.
- The [AT Protocol label specification](https://atproto.com/specs/label) supports treating labels as attributable claims, separate from the user's local policy for warning, hiding, blurring, downranking, or ignoring them.
