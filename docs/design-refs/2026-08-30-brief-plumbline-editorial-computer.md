# Brief: Plumbline editorial computer

## Status

Canonical product-derived brief for the shared Plumbline web shell at
`https://plumblines.uk/`.

## Product proposition

Plumbline is an AT Protocol social client that presents distributed services as
inspectable, replaceable parts of a user-controlled workbench. The visual
direction is **Seamful Hypertext Workbench**: a serious publication that became
interactive software.

## Required feeling

- Editorial, exact, calm, dense, and addressable.
- Warm paper surfaces framed by cool gray workspace and navy ink.
- Aged brass identifies Plumbline alignment and identity only.
- Rules, columns, and a plumb-line marker carry structure instead of decorative
  illustration.

## Inherited commitments

The shell inherits Edriffles Computer Web commitments to explicitness,
operator agency, visible state, learnable complexity, direct manipulation,
browser-native links, accessibility, responsive workbench layouts, Georgia-like
display type, Verdana-like interface type, monospace diagnostics, square
geometry, and structural shadows.

## Explicit exclusions

No beige wash, fake parchment, Victorian or newspaper cosplay, black-and-red
political imagery, circle-A symbols, Web3 or cypherpunk terminal styling,
generic SaaS card grids, fake desktop chrome, or protocol diagnostics as the
ordinary feed.

## Scope of this batch

Update the shared web shell boundary: canonical design tokens, first-paint
metadata, Plumbline masthead, desktop navigator and inspector framing, and
workbench document-stream surfaces. Preserve existing routes, provider
composition, OAuth, posting, engagement actions, and Spaces data behavior.

## Evidence mode

Product-derived. The complete visual direction and brand reference were
provided by the product owner; no external benchmark is needed to choose among
competing styles. Candidate exploration is therefore recorded as a locked
direction rather than used to invent alternate product identities.
