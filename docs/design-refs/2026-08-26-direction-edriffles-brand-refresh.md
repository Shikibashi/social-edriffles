# Direction: Edriffles Computer Web identity

## Chosen direction

Editorial nautical computer: a detailed ship-and-compass emblem sits inside
the existing ECW paper/grid system, while a quiet lowercase serif wordmark
anchors the product name. The identity should feel owned and specific without
turning ordinary social screens into a themed illustration.

## Rules

- Use the existing ECW navy, warm paper, purple accent, cyan link, and yellow
  focus roles; do not introduce a new gradient system.
- Use the source-owned Edriffles emblem for product marks and the lowercase
  `edriffles` wordmark for text lockups.
- Keep mark, wordmark, and descriptor distinct. Do not substitute a protocol
  namespace or provider badge for the product identity.
- Preserve square controls, structural borders, serif display type, readable
  sans-serif UI copy, and explicit focus treatment.

## Asset decision

The current companion-blog emblem and favicon set are the source of truth. The
historical Web99 mark and upstream Bluesky assets are not reused for the web
identity.
