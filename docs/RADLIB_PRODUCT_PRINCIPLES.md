# Radical-Liberal Product Principles

The term describes institutional allocation of authority, not political content promotion. The 24 owner principles are mapped in `artifacts/radlib-principle-traceability.json`.

The automated review treats **SATISFIED**, **PARTIAL**, **UPSTREAM-LIMITED**, **NORMATIVE-ONLY**, **VIOLATED**, and **NOT-IMPLEMENTED** as distinct. Partial transparency, explicit-vs-inferred ranking dominance, controlled serendipity, and live provider replacement remain qualified rather than being called satisfied. Owner approval is a separate manual decision.

Core invariants: individual control, content-neutral rules, pairwise nonassociation, third-party independence, explicit delegation/fallback, replaceable services and algorithms, user-owned credential-free personalization, truthful actor attribution, bounded engagement/serendipity, privacy minimization, reversible defaults, and no hidden meta-authority.
