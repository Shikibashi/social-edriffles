# Experimental Attention Modules v1

Constructive, Bridging, High Serendipity, Longform, and News are separately selectable, explicitly experimental options. Each has an independent versioned manifest, objective, limitations, and shared Candidate Protocol v1, Portable Personalization v1, Attention Constitution, and provider-security dependencies.

All modules are opt-in and deterministic, expose the common local ranking trace, and can exit to Balanced or chronological. Constructive uses inspectable conversation proxies; Bridging explores exposure diversity without compulsory ideological balancing; High Serendipity increases novelty while preserving hard constraints; Longform does not privilege a native record type; News emphasizes freshness, source/story diversity, provenance, and duplicate clustering. Provider-derived source reputation is evidence, not universal network trust.
